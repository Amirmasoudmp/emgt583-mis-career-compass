# EMGT 583 | MIS Career Compass

### [Open MIS Career Compass – Student Website](https://amirmasoudmp.github.io/emgt583-mis-career-compass/)

An interactive career preparation tool developed for EMGT 583 – Management Information Systems students at Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology.

Explore your MIS skills, generate personalized resume statements, prepare for interviews, and discover relevant career opportunities.

**Features:**
- MIS knowledge and skills self-assessment
- Entrepreneurial Mindset (3Cs) and Engineering Management habits
- Personalized resume and interview preparation
- Career exploration and LinkedIn job-search links
- Downloadable career takeaways

No student login required.
